PageChime – Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

PageChime is a reading journal and book inventory app for Android: it tracks the books and audiobooks you read, your reading progress and statistics, and the physical books on your shelves. This policy explains how the PageChime app (package com.pagechime.app), published by PageChime, handles your information.

In short: your library stays on your phone. The developer runs no servers, has no user accounts, and receives none of your data. To look up books, the app sends what you search for to the book catalogs Open Library and (only if you connect it) Hardcover, and audiobook titles to Libex and Audnexus to find their length. There are no ads and no analytics.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter – your books, reading progress (including the time of each update), ratings, reading history, tags, shelf locations, the condition, price and value of your copies, goals, and shelf arrangements – is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer cannot see it.

Importing, sharing, and the home screen widget

"Import from Goodreads or StoryGraph" reads the export file you pick on your device; the file is not uploaded anywhere. Year in review images are created on your device and are shared only when you tap Share and choose an app. The home screen widget shows your current book from the app's own storage.

Book lookups (Open Library, Hardcover, Libex and Audnexus)

When you search for a book, scan an ISBN barcode, open a book's series, or use "Update details" / "Update all book details", the app sends the search text, ISBN, or title and author of that book to Open Library, a service of the Internet Archive, and downloads book details and cover images from it. If you have connected Hardcover (see below), these lookups go to Hardcover first.

To find an audiobook's total length, the app sends the book's title and author to Libex (libexdb.com) and, using the Audible ID Libex returns, to Audnexus. Both are free, open services for audiobook information; no account is involved.

Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address and standard technical information with each request. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies: the Internet Archive's terms and privacy policy, Hardcover's policies, and those of Libex and Audnexus. The app does not send them anything else about you or your library.

Connecting Hardcover (optional)

You can connect a Hardcover account in Settings to get Hardcover's ratings and book details. You sign in on Hardcover's own website; the app never sees your Hardcover password. Hardcover gives the app a token that allows read-only access to its public book catalog – not your Hardcover library, profile, or activity. The token is stored encrypted on your device and is not included in device backups. Choosing "Disconnect" deletes it and revokes it with Hardcover.

Camera

The camera is used only when you open the barcode scanner, to read a book's ISBN. Camera images are analysed on your device and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Barcode reading uses Google's ML Kit, which runs on your device. ML Kit may send Google limited diagnostic information – such as device model, Android version, app version, a per-installation identifier, and performance metrics – to help Google improve it. It does not send camera images. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Backups

"Export backup" saves a file to a location you choose, such as your device or Google Drive; you control that file. If Backup is turned on in your device's settings, Android may also back up the app's library to your Google account, as it does for other apps; this is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • No ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • No account with the developer, and no data sent to the developer.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.

Deleting your data

You can delete books in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all data stored by the app on your device. Backup files you exported remain wherever you saved them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes – for example, if optional online features are added – the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
